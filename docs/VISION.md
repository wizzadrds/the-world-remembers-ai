# Vision

## One sentence

**THE WORLD REMEMBERS makes Minecraft remember.**

The player should initially feel like they are playing normal Minecraft. The first remarkable moment happens naturally: an NPC remembers a meaningful action from days earlier.

The technology stays invisible. Players experience consequences, relationships and stories rather than numerical reputation screens.

Long term, a player can help a villager, return weeks later, become part of their family's history, and eventually hear descendants describe what happened generations ago.

Villages remember wars, deaths, migrations, disasters and prosperity.

## Voice fantasy

Eventually the player can speak naturally to villagers. A busy villager may answer briefly; an available villager may continue the conversation; an annoyed villager may leave.

## What this is not

- Not simply ChatGPT inside Minecraft.
- Not a generic villager overhaul.
- Not a random dialogue generator.
- Not a voice-only mod.

The core product is persistent world memory and emergent behavior.
