# NPC Roles

Roles describe what an NPC is trained or expected to do. Roles are separate from personality.

## Initial roles

- CIVILIAN
- FARMER
- CRAFTSPERSON
- MERCHANT
- SCHOLAR
- WARRIOR
- VILLAGE_GUARDIAN

## Role + personality

A role never defines the whole character.

A warrior can be:

- brave and protective;
- timid and cautious;
- greedy and reluctant to risk equipment;
- social and eager to help;
- hot-tempered and likely to escalate conflict.

Two warriors should therefore behave differently even when they have the same equipment.

## Warriors

Warriors are intended to:

- equip armor and weapons;
- defend people and places;
- escort vulnerable NPCs;
- respond to threats;
- call for help;
- retreat when survival is more important than fighting.

They can become important characters with memories, relationships and possessions.

## Village guardians

Village guardians specialize in settlement defense.

Their behavior can prioritize:

- village boundaries;
- important buildings;
- vulnerable residents;
- alarm/help behavior;
- coordinated defense.

The guardian role is still affected by memory, relationships and personality.
