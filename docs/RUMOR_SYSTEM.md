# Rumor & Conversation System

## Goal
NPCs can only discuss facts grounded in persistent world state or memories. Knowledge has provenance and confidence.

## Knowledge origins
- DIRECT: witnessed or directly experienced.
- REPORTED: received from another NPC.
- RUMORED: propagated through multiple reports and therefore less certain.

## Degradation
Reported knowledge loses confidence over time. Rumored knowledge degrades faster. Repetition never creates truth.

## Conversations
Conversations are persistent records between NPCs. A conversation transfers a bounded set of grounded facts and records participants and tick.

## Truth model
Simulation state and persisted memories are authoritative. Dialogue is presentation, never world-state truth.
