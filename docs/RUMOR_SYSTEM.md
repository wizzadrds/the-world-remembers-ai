# Rumor & Conversation System

## Goal
NPCs can only discuss facts grounded in persistent world state or memories. Knowledge has provenance and confidence.

## Knowledge origins
- DIRECT: directly witnessed or experienced.
- REPORTED: received from another NPC.
- RUMORED: propagated through reports; confidence is lower.

## Degradation
Reported knowledge loses confidence with time; rumors degrade faster. Repetition never creates truth.

## Conversations
Conversations persist participants, tick and transferred facts. Transfers are bounded and grounded.

## Truth model
Simulation state and persisted memories are authoritative. Dialogue presents knowledge; it never creates world-state facts.
