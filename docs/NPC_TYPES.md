# NPC Types and Roles

The world should not contain one generic villager personality template.

## Archetypes

- CIVILIAN — ordinary resident.
- FARMER — values food, crops and routine.
- CRAFTSPERSON — values tools, materials and productive work.
- MERCHANT — values trade, reputation and valuable stock.
- SCHOLAR — values books, knowledge and exploration.
- WARRIOR — trained to fight and defend the settlement.
- VILLAGE_GUARDIAN — specialized defender with strong protection priorities.

An archetype is not a personality. Two warriors can be completely different people: one can be brave and friendly while another is timid and suspicious.

## Warriors

Warriors will have combat-capable behavior, equipment slots and protection priorities for their home, family and village.

They can eventually:

- equip armor and weapons;
- use shields and ranged weapons;
- escort villagers;
- defend important people and places;
- call other defenders for help;
- retreat when badly hurt, afraid or outmatched.

A warrior does not automatically attack everything hostile. Personality, fear, health, equipment, relationships and the importance of the threatened target all affect the decision.

## Equipment

Equipment is real state. Giving, losing, stealing, breaking, equipping or inheriting an item must correspond to an actual inventory/equipment transition.

Important possessions can become memories:

> “This sword was given to me by the player.”

That memory can later affect trust, affection, grief or protective behavior.

## Design rule

Role answers **what this NPC is trained to do**.

Personality answers **how this NPC tends to behave**.

Memory answers **what this NPC remembers**.

Relationships answer **who this NPC cares about or fears**.
