# Dream System

Dreams are consequences of remembered experience, not random AI text.

- Memory dreams surface important remembered events.
- Fear dreams reflect unresolved fear, loss, threat or stress.
- Nostalgia uses positive historical memories.
- Impossible dreams may violate physical reality while remaining anchored to real memories, people, places or relationships.

Dreams never rewrite world state. They are private by default, deterministic from saved inputs, bounded and rate-limited.
