# Dream System

## Goal
Dreams are a grounded narrative layer built from memories, fears, relationships and places the NPC actually knows.

## Principles
- Dreams never invent real-world facts as memories.
- A dream is explicitly marked as dream content and cannot silently become a factual memory.
- Dream selection is deterministic from NPC identity, world seed and sleep epoch.
- Strong memories and current stress influence dream themes.
- Fear dreams transform known threats; nostalgia revisits known people/places/items.
- Impossible dreams may violate world rules, but remain clearly non-factual.

## Dream types
- MEMORY: replay or recombination of remembered events.
- FEAR: threat-shaped dream driven by fear/stress and known dangers.
- NOSTALGIA: emotionally weighted reconstruction of family, home or lost relationships.
- IMPOSSIBLE: surreal recombination of known concepts with impossible outcomes.

## Safety of state
Dreams are ephemeral narrative outputs unless explicitly promoted by a later gameplay event. They must not mutate factual memory, family, inventory, village or relationship state by themselves.
